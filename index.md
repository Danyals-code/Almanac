# Almanac – Ancient Cycles

**Ancient calendars and the sky they were built to follow.**

Almanac names any day from 10,000 BC to AD 3000 the way the Maya, the Egyptians, and Chinese and Islamic astronomers counted it. It also works out when sixteen ancient sites, from Göbekli Tepe to Machu Picchu, line up with the sun, the moon and the stars.

- Turn the Maya, Egyptian, Islamic and Chinese calendar wheels with your finger.
- See every solar and lunar eclipse, and what you can see of it from where you are.
- Follow a site, such as Stonehenge or Newgrange, and get a reminder before its next alignment.
- Add widgets for a calendar wheel, an ancient site, or a sky compass.

Almanac works offline. It has no account, no ads and no tracking.
